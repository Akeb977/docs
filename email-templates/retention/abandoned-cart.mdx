# Abandoned Cart Email

Remind customers about items left in their cart and encourage them to complete their purchase.

## Strategy

Send a series of 3 emails:
1. **1 hour later**: Gentle reminder
2. **24 hours later**: Add urgency (limited stock, discount)
3. **72 hours later**: Last chance with incentive

## Email Sequence Strategy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Email 1 - Reminder (1 hour)">
    **Goal**: Gentle reminder without pressure
    - No discount
    - Helpful tone
    - Simple "You forgot something" message
  </Step>

  <Step title="Email 2 - Urgency (24 hours)">
    **Goal**: Add motivation with small discount
    - 10% discount code
    - Limited stock messaging
    - Social proof (if available)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Email 3 - Final Attempt (72 hours)">
    **Goal**: Last chance with best offer
    - 15% discount code
    - Expiration urgency
    - "Cart will be cleared" message
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices

<Tip>
  **Timing is Critical**: Send the first email within 1 hour for maximum recovery
</Tip>

<Warning>
  **Don't Overdo It**: 3 emails max. More feels spammy and reduces effectiveness
</Warning>

<Tip>
  **Test Discount Amounts**: A/B test 10% vs 15% vs free shipping to find what converts best
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Mobile Optimization**: 70% of cart abandonments happen on mobile - test thoroughly!
</Tip>

---

## Performance Metrics

Track these KPIs:
- **Recovery Rate**: % of abandoned carts that convert
- **Revenue Per Email**: Total recovered revenue / emails sent
- **Click-Through Rate**: % who click "Complete Order"
- **Time to Conversion**: How long after receiving email do they purchase

Average industry benchmarks:
- Recovery rate: 10-15%
- Open rate: 40-45%
- Click rate: 15-20%
