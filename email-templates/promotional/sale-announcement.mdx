## Email Copy

<Accordion title="Subject Lines">
  ```
  🔥 48-Hour Flash Sale: Up to 50% Off
  SALE ALERT: Your favorites are now on sale
  Don't miss this: [XX]% off everything
  Weekend Sale Starts Now! 🎉
  Ends Tonight: Extra 20% off sale items
  ```
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Copy Structure">
  ```
  1. Bold headline with discount %
  2. Sale duration (creates urgency)
  3. Key benefits/what's on sale
  4. Featured products
  5. Clear CTA
  6. Sale terms/exclusions (small print)
  ```
</Accordion>

## Sale Type Variations

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Flash Sale">
    - Duration: 24-48 hours
    - Higher urgency messaging
    - Countdown timers essential
    - Best for: Moving inventory fast
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Weekend Sale">
    - Duration: Friday-Sunday
    - More relaxed messaging
    - Focus on browsing experience
    - Best for: Regular promotions
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Seasonal Sale">
    - Duration: 1-2 weeks
    - Thematic imagery
    - Category-specific discounts
    - Best for: End of season clearance
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="VIP Early Access">
    - Duration: 2-3 days before public
    - Exclusive messaging
    - Loyalty rewards emphasis
    - Best for: Customer retention
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Design Tips

<Tip>
  **Use Bold Colors**: Red, orange, and yellow create urgency and attract attention
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Countdown Timers**: Add real-time countdown using your ESP's dynamic content
</Tip>

<Warning>
  **Don't Overuse**: Too many sales devalue your brand. Use strategically
</Warning>

<Tip>
  **Mobile-First Design**: 60%+ of sale emails are opened on mobile
</Tip>

## A/B Testing Ideas

Test these elements to optimize performance:

1. **Subject Lines**
   - Emoji vs no emoji
   - Discount percentage vs dollar amount
   - Urgency language variations

2. **Hero Image**
   - Lifestyle vs product-focused
   - Text overlay vs separate headline
   - Dark vs light background

3. **CTA Placement**
   - Hero only vs multiple CTAs
   - Button color variations
   - Button copy ("Shop Now" vs "Shop Sale")

4. **Discount Display**
   - "Up to X% off" vs "X% off everything"
   - Percentage vs dollar savings
   - Code required vs automatic

## Best Practices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Expectations">
    Clearly state sale duration and terms upfront
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Urgency">
    Use countdown timers and "while supplies last" messaging
  </Step>

  <Step title="Segment Your List">
    Send VIP/loyal customers early access
  </Step>

  <Step title="Mobile Optimize">
    Test on all major mobile email clients
  </Step>

  <Step title="Follow Up">
    Send reminder email 24hrs before sale ends
  </Step>
</Steps>
