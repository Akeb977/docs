## Assets Needed

<Steps>
  <Step title="Logo">
    Upload your logo to `/email-assets/logos/`
    - Format: PNG with transparent background
    - Recommended size: 300px x 100px (adjust ratio as needed)
    - Max file size: 50KB
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hero Image">
    Upload your hero image to `/email-assets/images/`
    - Format: JPG optimized
    - Size: 1200px x 600px (will scale to 600px width)
    - Max file size: 150KB
  </Step>

  <Step title="Social Icons">
    Upload social media icons to `/email-assets/icons/`
    - Format: PNG or SVG
    - Size: 64px x 64px (will display at 32px)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy Document">
    Save your finalized copy to `/email-assets/copy/welcome-email-copy.txt`
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Performance Tips

<Tip>
  **Optimize Images**: Use tools like TinyPNG to compress images by 50-70% without quality loss
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **A/B Test Subject Lines**: Try personalizing with first name or varying the discount mention
</Tip>

<Tip>
  **Send Timing**: Welcome emails perform best when sent within 5 minutes of signup
</Tip>
